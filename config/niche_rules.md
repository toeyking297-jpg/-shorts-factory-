# Channel rules for topic selection
- Faceless English YouTube Shorts, audience English speakers 18-45 (US/UK/CA/AU).
- Every Short reveals ONE surprising, TRUE fact and explains WHY.
- Must be verifiable with reliable sources (encyclopedia, official, major media, scientific bodies).
- Must have free visuals: real places, objects, nature, vehicles, space (NASA), maps/graphics.
- Economics is explained neutrally (geography, trade, history), never as investment advice or party politics.
- Avoid: politics and elections, war propaganda, religion debates, tragedy for shock value, gore,
  medical advice, conspiracy theories, unverifiable mysteries, content about private individuals, anything
  aimed at children.
